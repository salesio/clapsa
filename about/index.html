About CLAPSA

One Partner. Complete Procurement Integrity across Africa

Founded on the principle of making corporate and industrial purchasing effortless, CLAPSA Procurement has evolved into a premier supply partner for corporate enterprises, civil contractors, mining groups, and government agencies.

Company Purpose & Philosophy

One Source Supply Solutions for Africa's Foremost Enterprises

We bridge the gap between world-class trade manufacturers (Barron, Amrod, Altitude, Caterpillar, TOGS) and high-demand corporate clients by providing in-house branding precision, stringent SABS safety compliance, and seamless SADC cross-border logistics.

Our Mission

To eliminate supply chain bottlenecks for African corporations by delivering certified, high-grade uniforms, safety PPE, and branded promotional assets with zero defect tolerance.

Our Pan-African Vision

To be the most reliable, tech-driven corporate procurement partner across South Africa, Mozambique, Angola, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Zambia, and Namibia.

Certified Production Quality

Every garment, lanyard, and protective piece is inspected by in-house QA specialists prior to bulk cross-border dispatch.

In-House Production Power

Advanced Machinery & Manufacturing Facilities

We own and operate tier-1 production technology, allowing us to maintain stringent quality control, ultra-rapid sampling, and bulk capacity up to 50,000+ units.

Industrial Computerized Embroidery

High-density multi-head Japanese embroidery machinery delivering microscopic thread precision on blazers, caps, and jackets.

Wide-Format Dye Sublimation

Thermal calender presses creating rich, fade-proof colors on technical activewear, sports jerseys, and gazebos.

Screen Printing & Direct-to-Film (DTF)

Automated 8-color printing carousels utilizing eco-friendly plastisol and water-based inks with soft-hand finish.

Precision Laser Cutting & Engraving

CO2 and fiber laser engraving systems for executive metal pens, artisan hardwood coasters, and luxury plaques.

Certified PPE Testing & Compliance

Rigorous batch quality testing ensuring all safety footwear, goggles, and respiratory masks meet SABS standards.

SADC Fast-Track Freight Network

Bonded warehousing and direct road/air freight partnerships enabling 48 to 72-hour cross-border deliveries.
