Our 5-Step Turnkey Procurement & Quality Control

A streamlined, auditable process that guarantees precision brand fidelity, zero-defect manufacturing, and rapid freight across Southern Africa.

01

Brief & Requirements Analysis

Our technical consultants review your volume, technical specs, SABS standards, and brand guidelines to structure the optimal quotation.

02

Digital Proofing & Pre-Production Sample

We generate accurate 3D CAD renders and physical pre-production samples for client sign-off on fabric weight, color match, and stitching.

03

Precision In-House Manufacturing

Mass manufacturing runs on our automated embroidery carousels, wide sublimation heat presses, and screen-printing assembly lines.

04

Strict SABS & ISO Quality Inspection

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality checks for seam strength, embroidery tension, colorfastness, and safety certification.

05

SADC Express Freight & Handover

Carefully packaged, labeled by branch/employee, and dispatched with cross-border customs clearance across South Africa and SADC.
